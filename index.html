    How duels work
    • A duel starts only when the other person accepts. Turn off Available for duels and nobody can challenge you.
    • J or left click: Strike. K, Shift or hold right click: Guard, which takes 30% of a strike. Start guarding just before a strike lands to parry it: no damage, and the striker is staggered.
    • Duels happen on the office rooftop. W A S D move you, always facing the other one: close in to strike (strikes reach about 1.8 m), back off to make theirs miss.
    • Q: Focus Blast. Costs 50 mana and can't be guarded, but it flies to where they stood: step aside and it misses.
    • Weapons (Marketplace) change your strike: swords, a staff and a hammer reach further or hit harder; the pulse pistol and ion rifle fire bolts from across the helipad but slow you down. A bolt can be sidestepped, mostly stopped by a guard, or deflected by a perfectly timed one.
    • Gear tilts a fight, skill decides it: STR adds health and damage, AGI strikes faster and dodges more, INT strengthens Focus Blast, mana and critical hits.
    • A knockout wins. After 90 seconds, the higher health wins. Leaving for more than 10 seconds forfeits. No coins change hands.
